## CORRECTED tally — the first table's `fired` column was wrong

The original `fired` column counted only harness-track `patched-fuzz` firings and
**missed replay-track firings entirely**. Two rows (final30A Chart-19, night20c
Lang-63) showed `fired=0, judged=1`, which was the symptom. Both are replay-track
firings, judged and dismissed. Corrected counts now split the two tracks:

```
fired, ALL dismissed by judge   8
built but never fired           3
fired, mixed judge outcome      2
fired but never judged          1
died at construction            0   (every missed leg had >=3 accepted harnesses)

reaching a judge and being dismissed (wholly or partly):  10 of 14
```

The correction **strengthens** the headline: judge dismissal is 10 of 14, not 8,
and built-but-never-fired drops from 5 to 3.

## The dominant miss station is the JUDGE, not invention or construction

**10 of 14 misses reach a judge and are dismissed** (8 all-dismissed + 2 mixed).
Another 3 never fire. **Zero died at harness construction** — every missed leg
built at least 3 harnesses, and most built the full 5–7.

That is the opposite of where the live recall candidates aim:

* **8.5 (relation budget −m 12 vs −m 16)** targets invention volume. But no miss
  in this ledger died for want of built harnesses.
* **8.17 (deterministic invariant mining)** and **8.9 (family persistence)** target
  the invention lottery. The ledger cannot see that station directly (see the
  limitation below), but it can say that whatever was invented did get built.
* Nothing currently queued targets the largest class — **genuine catches dismissed
  by the judge.**

## Stated limitation — this ledger cannot see "never invented"

The plan asked for four stations including *family never invented*. This
classification is derived from accepted / fired / judged counts, so it reports the
**leg's** station of death, not the **winning family's** fate. A leg that built 5
harnesses of the wrong families and one that built 5 of the right ones both appear
as "built but never fired".

Distinguishing them requires knowing the winning family per leg, which is recorded
only for Chart-19 (`categoryplot-*`, from 2d) and Lang-63 (the month family, from
the three-roll decomposition `a75012d`). For the other 5 legs it is not known.

**So "died at construction = 0" is solid** (construction outcomes are counted
directly), but **"never invented = 0" is NOT claimed** — it is unmeasured, and the
5 built-but-never-fired legs are where it would hide.

## Consequences for the rungs below

1. **CORRECTED: this ledger does not kill 8.5's third arm or 8.17 — it defers them
   to 8.14b.** The first version of this consequence overreached. "Died at
   construction = 0" is a LEG-level count, and item 2d already measured, at FAMILY
   level, that Chart-19's winning `categoryplot-*` family died at harness
   construction 12-of-12 and 14-of-14, while Lang-63's family fate is a roll
   lottery (`a75012d`). Both known winning-family fates on record are
   invention-shaped. Since this ledger cannot see that station — by its own stated
   limitation — the correct action is the follow-up read (8.14b, winning-family
   fate per leg), not a kill. 8.5's third arm and 8.17 are re-gated on 8.14b.
2. **The judge-dismissal class deserves a lever and has none.** 8 of 14 misses are
   the judge dropping a firing on a genuinely fake patch. Cycle 7 measured the
   accusation side to a ceiling; this is the mirror — the **dismissal** side losing
   catches. Note these are *recall* losses from over-dismissal, whereas the ceiling
   work concerned *precision* losses from under-dismissal. The two pull in opposite
   directions, which is exactly why a single "judge harder/softer" knob cannot fix
   both, and why 5B (the dismissal lint) exists.
3. **The 5 built-but-never-fired legs are the reach problem** — harnesses exist,
   inputs never reach the discriminating state. That is the class seeds (item 6,
   killed on 7:1 exposure) and corpus work would target. Killed for precision cost;
   the ledger does not revive it, but it does size it at 5 of 14.
4. **8.5 keeps a narrower justification.** Its motivating observation (Chart-19
   caught at −m 16, missed at −m 12) is still worth one cheap test, but this ledger
   removes the general claim that budget starvation is a material miss class. Read
   it as a Chart-19-specific question, not a recall-lever test.
