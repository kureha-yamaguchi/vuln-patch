## What the dismissals actually say

**Category 1 dominates, and the refutations are good.** Examples, each citing
shown material:

* *Chart-19* — the check assumed `indexOf(null)` returns the index just `set`;
  the dismissal quotes the implementation's scan-from-zero loop, so a correct
  growable list legitimately returns an earlier null slot. The check is unsound.
* *Math-68, Math-73, Closure-92* — several dismissals cite
  `[fact:fires-on-both-confirmed]`: the same firing occurs on the unpatched
  build, so it is pre-existing surface, not evidence about the patch. That is the
  cycle-6 machinery working exactly as designed.
* *Closure-92* — the check demands one exact printed string; the dismissal shows a
  correct implementation may emit an optional trailing semicolon.
* *Closure-38* (2 of 4) — the check tests `contains("--")`, but the fuzzed
  variable name is literally `x--`, so the substring comes from the identifier.
  The check is confounded, and the dismissal says so.

**Category 2** covers dismissals on legs where 8.14c already showed the firing
family was not the winning one — Chart-19's `objectlist-*` checks (winner is the
`categoryplot` null-IAE property) and Lang-63's duration/period agreement checks
(winner is month non-negativity). Dismissing these is correct; the miss happened
upstream.

than overturning it. **Math-104's class is merited near-tolerance dismissal.** It
joins the parked tolerance-floor question. Honestly unwinnable at current
tolerance; the judge lever does not cover it.

## What this does to the recall picture

8.14 measured the judge as the dominant miss *station* (10 of 14). 8.18 measures
that station as **mostly correct behaviour**:

```
misses where the judge dismissed              ~10 of 14
   ... of those dismissals, merited or contested   32 of 34  (94%)
   ... genuinely wrong (ungrounded hypothetical)    2 of 34   (6%)
```

**So the dominant miss station is not a dominant miss cause.** The judge is where
misses become visible, but in 94% of dismissals it is dismissing something that
genuinely should not convict — an unsound check, a wrong-family check, a
pre-existing firing, or a near-tolerance artifact.

Combined with 8.14c (invention: 1 confirmed, ≤4) and 8.14's construction count
(zero), **cycle 8 has now measured every recall station and found no large,
addressable class.** The misses are distributed across small causes, most of which
are correct behaviour somewhere upstream or downstream.
