## The three ambiguous rows are themselves the finding

Per the pre-registered read-out: three ambiguous rows means **the archival gate is
at its resolution limit.** All three share one shape — a proposed rule sits on the
*same observable* with a *narrower or adjacent relation*:

* Chart-19: same method, different property (roundtrip vs null-rejection)
* Lang-63: same element, specific pin vs general invariant
* Lang-60: same query, non-mutation of *contents* vs of *capacity*

Whether those would have caught the bug is not answerable from proposal text — it
depends on what the generated harness actually exercised, which is the station
*after* invention. So the boundary between "invented" and "invented but too weak"
cannot be drawn from the archive.

**Consequence:** the invention items should not be gated on an archival read at
all. If 8.9 or 8.17 is to be justified, it needs a live A/B (does persistence /
mining change the catch rate?), not more reading. That is a stronger claim than
"unresolved" — it says this particular gate design cannot work.
