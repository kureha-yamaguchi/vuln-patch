# 8.14b — Winning-family fate per missed leg (raw table first, `1c9cd1d`)

The station 8.14 could not see. 8.14 classified the **leg's** death; this
classifies the **winning family's** fate, which is what gates the invention items.

## Method

*Winning family* = the check names whose alarm the judge **KEPT** on a roll where
that bug was **caught**. On a caught leg the kept alarm is the catch, so this is a
sound observational definition rather than a guess.

Then, for each missed roll: was any family with that shape proposed? Matching is
token-overlap (≥2 shared lowercase tokens between the winner's name and any
proposed name).

**Direction of the method's error:** loose matching produces false *present*, never
false *absent*. So the never-proposed count below is a **lower bound**.

## The gate is satisfied: never-invented IS a material class

At least **5 of 11 assessable misses** — and 5 is a floor, not a point estimate —
died because the family that catches the bug was never proposed that roll.

That re-licenses the invention items the corrected 8.14 deferred:

* **8.17 (deterministic invariant mining)** — its whole premise is that LLM
  synthesis re-rolls per leg and is the only candidate source. 5 of 11 is the
  measured size of that lottery.
* **8.5's third arm (focused synthesis)** — same station.
* **8.9 (family persistence)** — directly targets "proposed in one roll, absent
  the next", which is exactly Lang-60 (present in final30A where it was caught,
  absent in final30B and night20c where it was missed).

## Reconciling with 8.14 — the two readings are consistent

8.14 said 10 of 14 misses reach a judge and are dismissed. 8.14b says 5 of 11 had
no winning family proposed. These are not in tension: a leg can propose the wrong
families, build them, have them fire, and have the judge correctly dismiss them —
counting as "judge-dismissed" in 8.14 and "never proposed" in 8.14b.

**The correct joint reading:** the judge is where misses are *observed*, but for 5
of 11 the outcome was already determined upstream — the catching family was never
in the run, so no judging decision could have saved it. The judge class of 10 and
the invention class of 5 **overlap**; they are not additive.

That matters for lever choice. A judge-side fix cannot recover a leg whose
winning family was never proposed. An invention-side fix cannot recover a leg
whose family was proposed, fired, and dismissed on the merits.
