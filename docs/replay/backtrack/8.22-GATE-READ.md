## What roll A's 5-of-5 actually was

**Co-presence, not causation.** 8.4 puts raw values into the alarm text of every
normalizing check, so any dismissal of such a check quotes them whether or not
they did any work. The correlation is an artefact of the change being measured —
perfect correlation, absent mechanism. That is the exact trap this gate existed
to catch, and it caught it.

## The honest limit

Only ONE shared check was judged in both eras, so strand 1 rests on a single
comparison. Strands 2 and 3 do not depend on it, and "not confirmed" is the
conservative direction — it stops a build rather than licensing one. But that
single row must not be cited as though it were a population.
