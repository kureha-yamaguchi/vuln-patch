# Which prior offline claims the 201-char truncation touches. One grep, done.

Asked before 8.20 validates: do any prior offline studies read message CONTENT,
and do any of the 7 decisive-case rows sit among the 78 truncated?

## Answer: the decisive claim class is UNAFFECTED, and here is why

The truncation is confined to **one field**. Every evidence-bearing field is
whole:

```
  field                elided     max len
  fired_assertion      78/228        201     <-- the only one
  concrete_evidence     0/228      7,187
  code_context          0/228     47,913
  failing_test          0/228      4,211
  harness_source        0/228     18,806
```

The claim class flagged for checking — *"the evidence was present and ignored"* —
rests on `concrete_evidence` and `code_context`, **not** on `fired_assertion`.
Math-65 is the case that matters most and it settles it directly: its
`code_context` is **28,207 characters**, which is what the character-27,051 read
was performed against. Untruncated, and 12 of 12 Math-65 rows carry full
evidence and context regardless of whether their alarm headline was clipped.

So the judge, on every replayed row, saw the whole evidence block, the whole
code context, the whole failing test and the whole harness. What it saw clipped
was the alarm's own headline, on 78 of 228 rows.

## The decisive bugs ARE affected — in that one field

Worth stating rather than rounding away: **36 of the 78 truncated rows are on
the 7 decisive bugs.**

```
  Math-73 9 · Math-30 8 · Closure-62 7 · Lang-60 7 · Math-65 3
  Chart-26 1 · Math-39 1
  ...repairable from archive: 31        permanently truncated: 5
```

The 5 permanent ones include one Math-65 and one Math-39 row — the two
most-cited residuals. Those two rows' alarm headlines are prefixes forever.

## What each affected consumer can and cannot still claim

* **8.15 (authority) and 8.18 (dismissal categories)** — read judge OUTPUT
  blocks in the final30 traces, not fixture fields. Unaffected. (Note the
  nuance: a trace contains both capped alarm records and uncapped judge output;
  these studies read the latter.)
* **8.14 (miss ledger)** — final30A/B traces. Unaffected.
* **`verifier_replay`, therefore 8.1 part A and the noise floor** — passes
  `fired_assertion` to the judge (`verifier_replay.py:257`). Those runs judged a
  201-char prefix of the alarm on 34% of rows, with full evidence alongside.
  8.1's comparison is internally valid either way (both models read the same
  substrate), and its conclusion — same failure shape — stands. Its ABSOLUTE
  quality figures (80.3% / 81.7% and the rest) are measured on a partly-clipped
  alarm field and should be cited with that attached.

## Standing consequence

Anything replayed from now on should use `cases228_untruncated.jsonl` and
exclude the 8 flagged rows. The original stays for comparability with numbers
already recorded against it; it is not deleted and not corrected in place.
