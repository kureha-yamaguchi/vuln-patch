## Why this matters more than the blocked step

Without the assert, the pipeline would have been: extract (0 sources) → repair
(0 harnesses) → compile (0 pairs) → **"0 regressions, 0 new errors"** — a clean
pass, reported honestly, meaning nothing. That is the exact shape rule 15 names,
and it is the fourth instance this cycle after the empty failing-test block, the
terminal-marker substring, and the `family_duty(**kw)` stub.

## What covers the residual risk in the meantime

The step's purpose was to check the repair transforms are safe on crash-shaped
harnesses. Two pieces of evidence now stand in for it, and neither is complete:

1. **Compile safety is established on 111 semantic original/repaired pairs** —
   0 with more errors, 0 new error strings, against the real `jazzer-api` jar.
   The transforms are source-level and kind-agnostic, so this is relevant but not
   crash-specific.
2. **The crash-specific concern was cause-signature preservation**, and 8.12(a)
   now pins it by test: `repair_rethrow_without_cause` attaches the caught
   exception (feeding `cause_signature`'s `Caused by:` chain) and never strips an
   existing one. The exposure analysis had withdrawn this by reading; it is now
   withdrawn by test.

**Not covered:** whether crash-leg harnesses exhibit repairable defects at all,
and at what rate. That is unmeasurable from the archive.

## Consequence

8.12(b) is **blocked, not failed** — it should be re-run against a fresh corpus.

traces with `ACCEPTED`/`REJECTED` markers. So the dependency inverts: 8.12(b) now
runs *after* 8.12(c), from its output, rather than before it as a cheap
precondition.
